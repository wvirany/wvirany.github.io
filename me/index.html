About me

I'm a PhD student in computer science at the University of Toronto under the supervision of Dr. Michał Koziarski and Dr. Mike Brudno. My interests lie broadly in probabilistic machine learning and algorithmic decision-making with applications in science. In particular, I am currently focused on Bayesian optimization for drug discovery.

Before that, I received a B.S. in applied mathematics with minors in computer science and physics from the University of Colorado Boulder.
